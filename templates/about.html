About US

Our company proudly introduces SweetPOD, a premium Light Sugar Pod solution

designed to transform the coffee and beverage experience in Malaysia. Inspired by

Japan's highly successful coffee condiment culture, SweetPOD offers a more hygienic,

convenient, and premium alternative to traditional sugar sachets.

Through a strategic Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) partnership, we are

able to bring this innovative product to market quickly and efficiently without

requiring significant investment in manufacturing facilities. This approach allows us to

focus on product innovation, brand development, and market expansion.

Most importantly, SweetPOD aspires to become Malaysia's first recognisable liquid

sugar pod brand, pioneering a new category in the local beverage industry. We aim

to serve a wide range of market segments, including cafés, hotels, corporate offices,

vending machine operators, universities, airlines, and retail consumers.

By combining convenience, hygiene, and a premium user experience, SweetPOD is

more than just a sweetening solution—it represents a new and modern way for

Malaysians to enjoy their beverages. We believe SweetPOD has the potential to set a

new industry benchmark and redefine how sugar is served and consumed across

Malaysia.
